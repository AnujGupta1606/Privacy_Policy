Annadata – Child Safety Standards CSAE/CSAM

Last updated: 2026-03-25

Annadata is committed to protecting children and preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE), including child sexual abuse material (CSAM). We do not allow content or behavior that sexualizes, endangers, exploits, or abuses children.

Prohibited content & behavior

  • Any CSAM or content that depicts, promotes, or facilitates sexual exploitation or abuse of children.
  • Sexual content involving minors, grooming, solicitation, trafficking, or attempts to meet minors for sexual purposes.
  • Sharing, requesting, distributing, or linking to CSAM.
  • Accounts engaging in predatory behavior, harassment, coercion, or exploitation of minors.

Reporting child safety concerns

Users can report child safety concerns using the in-app reporting feature (where available). If you cannot access in-app reporting, you can email us at satyam2465@gmail.com.

Please include: your contact info, the account/content being reported, screenshots/links if possible, and why you believe it involves a child safety concern.

How we respond to reports

  • We review reports and may remove content, restrict features, suspend, or terminate accounts.
  • We preserve information as required for legal, security, and abuse-prevention purposes.
  • Where legally required and appropriate, we cooperate with and report to relevant regional/national authorities.
